LittleTales — Stories that come alive

Interactive read-aloud stories for children aged 4 to 10, in English and Arabic.

  • Listen and read along, with every word highlighted as it is spoken
  • Tap the pictures and emojis to make the story move and play
  • Tap any key word to hear it pronounced clearly
  • Stories about animals, adventure, bedtime and learning
  • AI story maker: parents type an idea and get a new, child-friendly story instantly
